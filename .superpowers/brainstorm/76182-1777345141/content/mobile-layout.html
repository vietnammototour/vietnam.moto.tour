Mobile Adaptations

  • Section reorder — on mobile, Pricing + Tour Details move up between Highlights and Itinerary (users want to see price early before scrolling through the full itinerary)
  • Included/Excluded — stacks vertically instead of side-by-side
  • Sticky bottom CTA bar — shows "From $XX" + WhatsApp/Email buttons, always visible while scrolling
  • Sidebar dissolves — all sidebar content flows into single column at appropriate positions
  • Timeline — stays the same, naturally fits narrow screens
  • Hero stats — wraps to 2 rows on small screens
