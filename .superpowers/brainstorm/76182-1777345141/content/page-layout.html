Layout Structure

  • Hero banner — title, breadcrumb, key stats (location, duration, distance, transport, rating), "from" price
  • Left column (~65%) — description, highlights (pill badges), itinerary (visual timeline), included/excluded (two-column list)
  • Right sidebar (~35%) — sticky: pricing card, WhatsApp + Email CTAs, tour details grid, payment terms, notes
  • Footer bar — meals info
  • Mobile — single column, sidebar content stacks below main content, pricing card becomes sticky bottom bar
