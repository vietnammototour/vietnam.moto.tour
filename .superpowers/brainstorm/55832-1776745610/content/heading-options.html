C — Grouped Heading Block

Treat label + heading as a single visual unit. Subtitle gets a left accent border for visual hierarchy. Fewer spacing decisions, stronger grouping.

Design principle: Elements that belong together should be closer to each other than to unrelated elements (Gestalt law of proximity). The label and heading are semantically one unit — their spacing should reflect that.
